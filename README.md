## 4. Ad Filtering & Content Blocking

OpenCUY includes features that detect and block certain content:

- **"Free with ads" badges** — The tool attempts to detect and avoid videos labeled as ad-supported.
- **Live streams** — Live broadcasts are blocked by default.
- **New videos** — Videos uploaded less than 24 hours ago are blocked by default.

These features are **user-configurable** and can be disabled in the Config tab. The authors make **no guarantees** about the accuracy, completeness, or reliability of these filters. Content may be misclassified, and legitimate content may be blocked unintentionally.

**Respect content creators.** Ad revenue is a primary income source for many creators. Circumventing ads deprives them of rightful compensation. Use this software responsibly and consider supporting creators through legitimate means (e.g., YouTube Premium, Patreon, direct donations).

## 5. Browser Automation Risks

This software uses **Selenium** to automate a Google Chrome browser instance. Be aware of the following risks:

- **Account flagging** — Automated browsing patterns may be detected by YouTube/Google, potentially leading to account restrictions.
- **CAPTCHA challenges** — Automated access may trigger CAPTCHAs or other verification mechanisms.
- **IP blocking** — Excessive or suspicious activity may result in temporary or permanent IP blocks.
- **Data exposure** — Cookies, session data, and browsing history may be accessible to the automated browser. The default configuration disables cookies and uses a temporary profile, but this can be changed.

**You assume all risks** associated with browser automation.

## 6. Live Chat & Command Execution

The live chat feature reads messages from YouTube live streams and executes commands based on a configurable prefix (default `!`). This means:

- **Anyone in chat** who knows the prefix can potentially trigger commands.
- **Commands execute in your browser** with your session's permissions.
- **Malicious actors** could abuse this to navigate your browser, change playback settings, or perform other actions.

**Do not connect to untrusted live chats.** The authors are not responsible for any actions taken by third parties through the live chat command interface.
